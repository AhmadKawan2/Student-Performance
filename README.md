# Student-Performance data set

#Introduction:
Student Performance Dataset
In contemporary educational research, predictive analytics and data mining play a crucial role in identifying the factors that influence academic success. This dataset provides a comprehensive look at the academic, socio-economic, and demographic profiles of students across various districts in Bangladesh. By capturing a mix of institutional factors, home environments, and individual student behaviors, the dataset serves as a powerful foundation for understanding and predicting student outcomes.
